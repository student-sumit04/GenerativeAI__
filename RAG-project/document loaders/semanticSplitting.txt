Semantic meaning of the text is important for the embeddings and retrieval in RAG. The semantic splitting method is based on the meaning of the text rather than just characters or tokens. This method helps to create more accurate embeddings by ensuring that each chunk represents a clear idea or topic. 

When using semantic splitting, it is important to consider the context and relationships between sentences and paragraphs. This can be achieved through natural language processing techniques that analyze the structure and meaning of the text.
